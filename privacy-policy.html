Information voluntarily shared by email may be used to reply to your message, discuss a project, or provide requested support. It is not sold through this website.

Links on this website may take you to third-party services, such as email providers, GitHub, LinkedIn, or Google Play. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Android Applications

Individual Android applications may have their own privacy policies depending on their permissions, features, third-party services, and data handling. Please review the privacy policy shown on the relevant app listing or inside the app for details that apply to that application.
